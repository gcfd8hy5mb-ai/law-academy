# Level 3 — Final Examination Calibration and Human Examiner Protocol
**Status:** draft examiner aid; not an accreditation, independent academic evaluation, or formal scoring result.  
**Applies to:** L3-1-10 through L3-7-10 (seven distinct fictional final cases).

## What the examiner receives
1. The student’s 2,000–2,500-word memorandum, adverse position, decision tree, evidence chart, five-minute oral outline, citation/authority log, and self-review.
2. The unmodified fictional case file and its three prescribed changes of facts.
3. The relevant course’s built-in `examRubric` (five areas, 100 points total).
4. Independent access to statutes, court rules, official opinions and a reliable treatment/citator resource. **A hyperlink is not a substitute for checking current law.**

## Core scoring matrix
| Criterion | Points | Full-credit performance | Low-credit warning |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Issue spotting and correct legal framework | 25 | Complete and prioritized issues, correct law for named jurisdiction and time | Invented tests, omitted dispositive issue or misidentified source hierarchy |
| Application to factual record | 30 | Element-by-element, adverse facts, burden, alternative findings, explicit uncertainty | Restates rules, assumes unproven facts, unsupported numerical conclusions |
| Counterarguments and fact changes | 15 | Strong opposing theory and results under all three specified changes | Strawman response or no change-in-outcome analysis |
| Source accuracy and jurisdiction | 20 | Verifiable authorities, current text, holdings, subsequent treatment and court hierarchy | Fabricated or materially misquoted authority, historical rule treated as universally current |
| Organization and professional judgment | 10 | Coherent recommended ruling, defensible remedy and precise limitations | No resolution, unclear parties or wrong requested relief |

**Scoring:** Mark each criterion 0 through its allotted points and provide 2–4 written sentences of feedback per criterion. Scores below are proposed internal standards, **not a university-approved grading policy**. A student cannot receive a recorded professional mastery approval solely through a chatbot or quiz.

## Proposed calibration bands (requires adoption by qualified reviewer)
- **90–100:** sophisticated; only nonmaterial errors, complete authority and counterargument analysis.
- **80–89:** strong; some minor gaps with correct controlling framework.
- **70–79:** developing; significant omissions requiring targeted rewrite before mastery.
- **Below 70:** not ready; retake after remediation.

**Automatic manual-review flags (not an automatic grade):** any invented court citation; applying the wrong jurisdiction’s rule without explanation; treating secondary guidance as a holding; invented numerical facts that are necessary for the outcome; failure to distinguish procedural posture or controlling changes in law; plagiarism; or substantive conclusion that depends on an unverifiable source. Log the issue and the actual impact on the rubric, without automatically assigning zero to the entire paper.

## Course-specific examiner spot checks
| Final | Mandatory issues to check | Common false shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence (L3-1-10) | Rule 901 video authentication, each Rule 805 hearsay layer, Rule 702 as amended, Rule 104(a), impeachment and preservation | Treating a medical record or an expert's credentials as automatically admitting every statement |
| Business Organizations (L3-2-10) | CEO authority; transaction date and Delaware conflict statute; demand-futility analysis; direct versus derivative injury; separate LLC law | Assuming director abstention cures loyalty concerns or corporate law governs every LLC claim |
| Administrative Law (L3-3-10) | Standing, finality, APA §§553/706, independent statutory interpretation under *Loper Bright*, separate review of the agency's record | Automatic Chevron deference or treating every agency message as an enforceable rule |
| Professional Responsibility (L3-4-10) | Identity of client, privilege owner, concurrent/former-client conflicts, candor, fictitious citations and appropriate corrections | Assuming corporate counsel necessarily represents the CEO personally |
| Federal Taxation (L3-5-10) | Explicit tax year, basis and allocation unknowns, installment sale, §1091 wash-sale facts, placed-in-service and election rules | Inventing basis or importing another tax year's depreciation threshold |
| Remedies/Litigation (L3-6-10) | Contribution-margin damages, mitigation, narrow Rule 65 relief, settlement jurisdiction under *Kokkonen* | Treating gross revenue as lost profit or assuming dismissed settlements remain under federal jurisdiction |
| Criminal Procedure (L3-7-10) | *Riley*, Miranda, post-charge Sixth Amendment attachment and elicitation, Brady/Giglio, separate remedies | Conflating questioning doctrines or treating every discovery delay as automatic suppression |

## Three anchor responses for blind examiner calibration
Use a **new fictional answer written by the reviewing instructor** for each relevant course, not a student's private work. Two qualified reviewers independently grade each anchor and reconcile differences exceeding 10 total points or 5 points in one criterion.

**Anchor A — strong (expected 90–100):** Identifies every threshold issue and burden, separates adverse facts from assumptions, checks primary controlling law and later treatment, handles all three fact changes, states missing evidence, and writes a narrowly tailored ruling. The numerical result is never invented where inputs are absent.

**Anchor B — borderline (expected 70–79):** Spots the principal claims and cites some relevant sources but misses a key alternate procedure, weakens one counterargument, does not resolve one prescribed fact change, and omits a material limitation. Examiner identifies the smallest remediation needed and scores accordingly.

**Anchor C — weak (expected below 70):** Recites legal labels without applying facts, uses nonexistent cases, treats all jurisdictions identically, assumes facts that decide the case, or lacks the assigned adverse argument. Examiner specifies corrective research and reassessment rather than equating an unverified automated score with competency.

These anchors are **descriptions of calibration samples**, not actual graded candidate responses.

## Reviewer record
For each final, capture reviewer name, credentials and jurisdiction; examination ID; law-as-of date; controlling sources checked; criterion-by-criterion score; observed source and reasoning errors; remediation; second-reader decision where applicable; final approval date. Store *no* personally identifiable student answers in public GitHub.

## Completion gate
Do not switch any Level 3 course to “verified,” “accredited,” “independently reviewed,” or “mastered” on the strength of this protocol alone. Requires a qualified human examiner, completed case-law currency review, reliable scoring calibration, passing test suite and browser/persistence checks.
