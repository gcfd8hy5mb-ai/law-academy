## 1L Torts — L2-2-4: Defamation and privileges

Opinions:
- *New York Times Co. v. Sullivan*, 376 U.S. 254 (1964): https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/376/254
- *Milkovich v. Lorain Journal Co.*, 497 U.S. 1 (1990): https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/497/1

Under *Sullivan*, a public official's recovery for defamatory statements about official conduct requires knowing falsity or reckless disregard of truth, a legal concept of actual malice distinct from personal ill will. *Milkovich* refuses a wholesale separate constitutional opinion privilege when a statement implies provably false facts. The new exercise separates a blogger's statement about an official from a private employer reference, adding a publication/fault/privilege evidence table and paired written pleadings. **Limit:** Neither opinion determines the actual facts or applicable private-person state-law burden; subsequent treatment remains to be checked.

## 1L Criminal Law — L2-4-4: Self-defense and justification

Texas Penal Code §§9.31–9.32: https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/PE/htm/PE.9.htm

Nondeadly and deadly force have distinct statutory requirements, particularly reasonable belief in immediate necessity. Retreat rules and conditional statutory presumptions do not automatically legalize deadly force if foundational requirements are missing. The new timed confrontation problem tests perceived threat, provocation, withdrawal, admissible evidence and competing narratives. **Limit:** Jurisdiction-specific and factual; not advice on using force.

## 1L Constitutional Law — L2-5-4: Procedural due process

*Mathews v. Eldridge*, 424 U.S. 319 (1976): https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/424/319

The three factors are the affected private interest, risk of mistaken deprivation and probable value of additional procedures, and government interests/costs. The Court found the specified procedures sufficient for Social Security disability benefit termination; it did not announce that no hearing can ever be required before any government deprivation. The new exercise applies the balance to a mistaken-identity vendor permit case and compares pre- and postdeprivation procedures. **Limit:** Actual existence of a protected entitlement and any specific statutory procedure need separate authority.

## 2L Evidence — L3-1-2: Digital authentication

Federal Rules of Evidence 901 and 902(13)–(14):
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_901
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_902

Rule 901 addresses authentication; Rules 902(13)–(14) permit certain qualified certifications concerning electronic process records and digitally identified copies with applicable procedural notice. A certified file is not automatically attributable to a named author or free of hearsay objections. The new shared-device lesson separately evaluates authenticity, identity/attribution and admissibility, with written foundation and evidentiary ruling exercises. **Limit:** No actual forensic evidence or digital certification was obtained; facts are fictional.
