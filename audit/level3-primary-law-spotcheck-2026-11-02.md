## Evidence — Federal Rule of Evidence 702
- Primary text and official amendment context: https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-evidence
- Readable operative rule and 2023 amendment note: https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_702
- Spot-check finding: rule expressly requires the proponent to demonstrate to the court, more likely than not, that the requirements for expert testimony are met. Expert opinion must reflect reliable application of methods to case facts. Not every sufficiency or reliability challenge automatically goes to weight. Reading `L3-1-4` was clarified to state this. The other Evidence lessons and each citation still require a full source-by-source audit.

## Administrative Law — Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo
- Primary Supreme Court opinion, decided June 28, 2024: https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-451_7m58.pdf
- Spot-check finding: `L3-3-1` and `L3-3-2` appropriately distinguish independent judicial interpretation of federal statutes after Chevron was overruled from discretion lawfully delegated to agencies. The court cannot defer solely because a statute is ambiguous. This does not independently establish completeness of the courses or subsequent case treatment.
