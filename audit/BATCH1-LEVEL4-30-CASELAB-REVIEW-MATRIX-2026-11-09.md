## Integrity rules

- Record-level “screened” means only the named fields were inspected; it is not the same as correct, verified, graded or accepted.
- An improved citation list or an answered quiz does not establish a complete lesson legal accuracy pass.
- Do not set `legalPropositionsVerified`, `fullPrimarySourcesChecked`, `subsequentTreatmentChecked`, `jurisdictionAndEffectiveDateChecked`, `practicalAssessmentHumanGraded`, or `independentReviewerApproved` to true without evidence for the particular item.
- Branch edits require review, test execution and merge before production deployment; no new production QA claim is made here.
