## 1L Civil Procedure: discovery, summary judgment (`L2-3-2`)
- Rule 26(b)(1): relevance, privilege and proportionality; Rule 34(b): reasonably particular descriptions and specific objections; Rule 37(e): lost ESI, reasonable preservation efforts, possibility of restoration, prejudice, and separate intent finding for severe sanctions.
- Rule 56(a),(c),(d),(e): no genuine dispute as to material facts, supporting record materials, unavailable facts and remedies for unsupported factual positions. Nonresponse does not mechanically entitle a movant to judgment.

## 1L Civil Procedure: removal and remand (`L2-3-5`)
- Official 28 U.S.C. §§1441, 1446, 1447; removal requires an existing jurisdictional basis; procedural notice, consent, timing, forum-defendant limitation and remand grounds are distinct tests.
- Section 1446(b)(2)(A) requires properly joined and served defendants' consent in §1441(a) removals; §1446(c)(1) has a one-year diversity limitation **in the circumstances covered by §1446(b)(3)**, subject to a bad-faith exception. Do not generalize that exception beyond its statutory text.
- Section 1447(c) ordinarily requires a motion for remand for defects other than subject-matter jurisdiction within 30 days after notice of removal, whereas lack of subject-matter jurisdiction may require remand any time before final judgment.
- Official texts: https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t28/s1441/ ; https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t28/s1446/ ; https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t28/s1447/
- In the hypothetical Texas-versus-Texas citizenship and $60,000 claim, no basic diversity basis exists. The forum-defendant rule is a further distinct question, not a substitute for establishing original jurisdiction.
