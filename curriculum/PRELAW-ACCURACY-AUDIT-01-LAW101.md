## Source-to-claim checks
1. **Federal jurisdiction** — U.S. Courts, *Types of Cases*: https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/types-cases . 28 U.S.C. § 1332: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1332&num=0&edition=prelim . U.S. District Court, Maryland, *Complaint — Basis for Jurisdiction*: https://www.mdd.uscourts.gov/sites/mdd/files/forms/Complaint.pdf (illustrates federal-question/diversity and complete diversity).
2. **Criminal cases and burden** — U.S. Courts, *Criminal Cases*: https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/types-cases/criminal-cases
3. **Civil cases and burden** — U.S. Courts, *Civil Cases*: https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/types-cases/civil-cases
4. **Summary judgment** — *Anderson v. Liberty Lobby, Inc.*, 477 U.S. 242 (1986), full opinion: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/477/242 . The hypothetical needs separate premises-liability authority from the relevant state.
5. **Supremacy / source hierarchy** — Constitution, Art. VI cl. 2, https://constitution.congress.gov/browse/article-6/clause-2/ . Public constitutional teaching is not a substitute for verifying an actual federal preemption argument.
6. **Legal reasoning frameworks** — IRAC/FIRAC are pedagogical methods, not controlling legal rules; their definitions were editorially checked, not marked independently legally certified.
