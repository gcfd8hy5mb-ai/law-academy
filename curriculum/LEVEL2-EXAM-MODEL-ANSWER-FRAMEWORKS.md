# Level 2 Timed Writing Exam — Post-Submission Model-Answer Frameworks
**Status:** educational reviewer guidance; not an independent legal accuracy certification or official bar exam answer key. This resource is deliberately stored apart from the live student prompts and should only be provided after the student submits their attempt.

## Contracts
**Issue map:** Is the transaction a sale of goods governed by UCC Article 2? Did the parties form a contract? Are changed acknowledgment terms part of the agreement (including merchant-to-merchant issues)? What are the material delivery and performance obligations? What damages are recoverable and proved?
**Analysis:** Identify precise offer/acceptance and any conflicting or additional terms; distinguish formation under UCC § 2-207 from later breach and remedies. Assess notice of objection, the nature of the modification, actual acceptance, consequential loss and proof/foreseeability. Do not infer an automatic right to lost profits.
**Excellent answer:** Separates alternative legal outcomes based on jurisdiction-specific UCC enactment and factual gaps, and fairly states seller's defenses.

## Torts
**Issue map:** Duty and premises liability standard, actual/constructive notice, reasonable response, breach, cause-in-fact, scope of liability, damages, comparative fault.
**Analysis:** Notice of a known dangerous condition is evidence, not automatic liability. Competing causes must be addressed; seek witness, inspection and maintenance records. Apply the state's rules for visitor status and comparative responsibility.
**Excellent answer:** Explains why causation and breach cannot be merged and proposes targeted discovery.

## Civil Procedure
**Issue map:** Subject-matter jurisdiction, citizenship of parties, amount in controversy, personal jurisdiction, venue and any transfer or dismissal motions.
**Analysis:** A qualifying amount plus diverse citizenship may create original diversity jurisdiction, but does not establish personal jurisdiction or venue. Identify corporation/LLC citizenship correctly, assess claims and parties separately, and require minimum-contacts facts.
**Excellent answer:** Provides a structured sequence: jurisdiction, pleading, service, defenses and forum alternatives.

## Criminal Law
**Issue map:** Act, statutory intent to deprive, knowingly taking another's property, mistake of fact, evidentiary conflicts and burden of proof.
**Analysis:** Compare defendant's stated belief with observed conduct and alleged admissions. A factual mistake may negate a required mental element depending on the statute; distinguish subjective intent from proof of intent and do not shift the prosecution's burden.
**Excellent answer:** Analyzes both acquittal and conviction theories without inventing facts.

## Constitutional Law
**Issue map:** State action, nature of university-controlled forum, scope of student-group access, viewpoint discrimination, neutral safety justifications and relief.
**Analysis:** University action raises First Amendment issues, but forum category and policies matter. Comparator events may undermine the neutral explanation; safety concerns still warrant factual evaluation. Distinguish content discrimination from viewpoint discrimination.
**Excellent answer:** Evaluates government's strongest argument alongside unequal treatment.

## Property
**Issue map:** Prior unified ownership, severance, access at severance, necessity, existing prior use, express easement and alternative routes; scope and possible prescription.
**Analysis:** Easement by necessity and easement by prior use have different elements. Expensive alternative access may affect degree of necessity; jurisdiction controls. Seek deed chain, maps, historical route use and physical access evidence.
**Excellent answer:** Applies each theory separately and avoids asserting that landlocked status automatically creates a right-of-way.

## Legal Research & Writing
**Issue map:** Exact jurisdiction, statute and amendment effective date, industry exception, pre-amendment case validity, disputed client facts and administrative procedures.
**Analysis:** Research official legislation and codification, check later opinions and negative treatment, and distinguish what precedent still governs after amendment. Structure objective memo with question, brief answer, facts, discussion and qualified conclusion.
**Excellent answer:** Provides dated source log and candidly identifies facts that would change the recommendation.

## Grading controls
Use the separate 100-point rubric: issues 20, accurate law/authority 25, application/counterarguments 30, conclusion/uncertainty 15, organization/citation 10. A multiple-choice check or keyword match cannot replace substantive essay grading. Do not assign an automatic pass or bar-readiness label. A reviewer should document legal sources, jurisdiction, feedback, date and their qualifications.
