## Delivered in this commit

- Each lesson presents doctrine, a realistic hypothetical, an independent authority-checking task, a short written IRAC prompt, and one formative knowledge check

- The app remains a separate project from Business Academy; no schema or progress-storage keys changed

## Study requirements for the independent-reading exercises
1. Identify the legal issue and jurisdiction before selecting authorities.
2. Consult the source linked inside the lesson, including date and subsequent developments.
3. Summarize the rule in your own words, noting material limitations.
4. Apply each rule element to given facts and make a plausible counterargument.
5. Write a qualified conclusion explaining factual and legal uncertainty.

## Quality and completion limitations
These lessons are beginner introductions, not complete university-equivalent courses. Time labels are suggested guided-study targets, **not verified delivered instructional hours**. Source reading, written IRAC work, and later legal-case interpretation require substantive assessment; the app currently does not automatically grade their legal accuracy. Existing quizzes and cumulative exams are formative, not proof of program completion. Later batches should introduce validated source-linked casebooks, richer original hypotheticals, separate multi-question assessments, writing-rubric evaluation, and iPhone browser testing. Do not award Level 1 graduation based only on a single quiz or the current cumulative practice examination.
