# Pre-Law Level 1 — Completion Criteria and Evidence

Updated 2026-10-31. Supersedes the outdated 37-lesson inventory. **There are 85 authored lessons in six Pre-Law courses.**

## Student learning checkpoints
1. Complete all **85** lesson knowledge checks; saved progress is formative evidence only.
2. Attempt and pass each of six course practice exams at the application's stated 80% threshold, then the cumulative practice exam at 80% or higher. These are practice checks and should not be represented as proctored finals.
3. Complete the six-course written final, the six applied legal analysis scenarios, substantive writing assignments, and the research capstone. Preserve drafts and source logs; do not infer substantive proficiency from saved text alone.
4. Produce at least three substantial written analyses (400+ words as a drafting floor, not proof of quality), with citations, opposing arguments, and expressly identified uncertainty.
5. Have a qualified independent legal reviewer evaluate law/source currency and legal application using a dated rubric. Arrange an independently assessed issue-spotting/oral defense if academic sign-off is sought.

## Curriculum development status versus student completion
**Authored:** 85 foundational lessons, practice assessments, written finals and six applied scenarios. 
**Technical testing:** Automated content and mobile browser tests operate in GitHub Actions. Latest pass must be checked against the commit being approved.
**Not yet academically signed off:** Full lesson-by-lesson doctrinal verification of all 85 lessons, all quiz answer keys, citation currentness, and independent evaluation. Existing targeted source-review batches are not substitutes for this review.
**Graduation:** Remains intentionally locked. No academic credit, accredited law-school award, or license is promised.

## Reviewer record
Use `PRELAW-REVIEWER-ANSWER-FRAMEWORKS.md` and `PRELAW-SIX-COURSE-APPLIED-ASSESSMENTS.md`. Record jurisdiction, controlling primary sources, access/review date, issue-spotting, counterarguments, case application, and corrections; do not mark a lesson legally verified solely because a source URL exists.

## Integrity and privacy
Work is educational, not legal advice. Respect confidentiality: do not enter a real client's identifiable matter into public examples or untrusted submission channels. Export drafts for review as appropriate. Keep local storage keys compatible with existing users. Do not award completion based on keyword checks, minimum word count, self-score, or clicking buttons.
