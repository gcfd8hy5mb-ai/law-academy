# Level 1 Readiness and Graduation Gate

The app now displays seven checkpoints on More → My Progress:
1. All 37 existing lesson checks passed (automatically tracked).
2. All six course practice exams passed at 80%+ (automatically tracked).
3. Cumulative 12-question practice exam at 80%+ (automatically tracked).
4. Drafts saved for all current writing assignments (automatically tracked).
5. At least three drafts of 400+ words (length only; automatically tracked).
6. Independent evaluation of legal correctness and source accuracy (not yet implemented).
7. Final evaluated oral/issue-spotting assessment (not yet implemented).

**Graduation remains locked**, regardless of automatic checkpoint state. Short exams, keywords, word counts and self-ratings are not substantive validation of a lawyer's legal knowledge. The app offers no accredited credential or bar eligibility.

All prior local progress and writing storage keys remain unchanged. The checklist links back to Courses, Exams, and Writing Lab to help the learner find relevant work.

Future requirements: accurate case citations and currency checks; substantive written rubrics; evaluation by a qualified legal reviewer; robust, longer problem sets; accessible on-device testing; explicit assessment integrity policy.
