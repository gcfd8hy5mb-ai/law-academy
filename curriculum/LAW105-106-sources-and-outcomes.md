# LAW 105–106 Introductory Curriculum

Status: four introductory lessons each in Everyday Legal Problems and Introduction to Legal Ethics, with formative single-question checks. Not an accredited or J.D.-equivalent course.

## Subject references
- [Cornell Legal Information Institute](https://www.law.cornell.edu/) for legal definitions and jurisdiction-based research.
- [ABA Model Rules of Professional Conduct](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/) for model ethical standards. Model Rules are not automatically controlling; check the relevant jurisdiction's adopted rules.
- [U.S. Courts](https://www.uscourts.gov/) for federal judicial process and court educational resources.
- [National Center for State Courts](https://www.ncsc.org/) for state court systems.

## Outstanding academic work
Create multi-question assessments, written case analysis, realistic ethical dilemmas, instructor-like guided feedback, and source checks. Laws and ethics requirements vary by jurisdiction and date. No legal advice is provided.
