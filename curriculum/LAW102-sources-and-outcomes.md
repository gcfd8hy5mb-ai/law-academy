# LAW 102 — Constitutional Foundations

Status: first four introductory lessons implemented; this is **not a complete law-school constitutional law course**.

## Learning outcomes
- Distinguish Articles I, II, III and identify separation of powers.
- Analyze federalism, federal preemption, and the Supremacy Clause at an introductory level.
- Recognize the government-action question and incorporation of rights.
- Brief the jurisdiction and judicial-review issues in *Marbury v. Madison* (1803).

## Authoritative introductory references
- National Archives, [The Constitution: What Does It Say?](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution/what-does-it-say)
- Library of Congress, [Constitution Annotated](https://constitution.congress.gov/)
- U.S. Courts, [Supreme Court Landmarks](https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/educational-resources/supreme-court-landmarks)
- Federal Judicial Center, [Marbury v. Madison](https://www.fjc.gov/history/cases/cases-that-shaped-the-federal-courts/marbury-v-madison)
- National Archives, [Bill of Rights](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights)

Read and link to originals; the lessons use newly written summaries rather than importing protected casebook text.
