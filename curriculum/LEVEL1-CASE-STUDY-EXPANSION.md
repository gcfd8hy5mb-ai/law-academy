# Level 1 — Supreme Court Case Study Expansion

Added original assignments using public judicial opinions or official historical records:
- Brown v. Board of Education, 347 U.S. 483 (1954), National Archives: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/brown-v-board-of-education
- Miranda v. Arizona, 384 U.S. 436 (1966), Cornell LII: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/384/436
- New York Times Co. v. Sullivan, 376 U.S. 254 (1964), Cornell LII: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/376/254
- Comparative three-case research memorandum with official Supreme Court opinions directory: https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/opinions.aspx

All four tasks ask students to read the source, identify material facts, issue, holding, reasoning, jurisdictional and historical limits, and write a substantial response in their own words. These tasks link to opinions rather than copying full external source text. Course pages now link directly to Case Studies & Writing.

**Quality gate:** No independent legal review or AI grading is provided. Writing rubrics are self-assessment tools. These assignments are not yet a full accredited 1L course or evidence of legal practice competence. Verify later legal developments before applying historical doctrines to new facts.
