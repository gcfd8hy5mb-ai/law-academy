# Law Academy — Five-Level Bar-Readiness Academic Standard
Established: 2026-10-31. Purpose: make **demonstrated readiness for the actual bar examination** the measurable capstone goal of this independent education app, without claiming official exam eligibility, accredited education, or guaranteed passage.

## Verified examination target
Texas adopts the NextGen Uniform Bar Examination beginning July 2028. The official NCBE blueprint controls, not an assumed fixed historical MBE/MEE syllabus. Sources:
- https://www.ncbex.org/exams/nextgen/content-scope
- https://www.ncbex.org/exams/nextgen/about-nextgen

NCBE's July 2028 foundational subject list includes business associations, civil procedure, constitutional law, contracts, criminal law/procedure, evidence, family law, real property and torts. Its foundational skills are legal research, writing, issue spotting/analysis, investigation/evaluation, client counseling/advising, negotiation/dispute resolution, and client relationship/management. Keep the blueprint version, last verified date and Texas requirements auditable.

## Five-level learning architecture
1. **Pre-Law**: six introductory courses; accurate source hierarchy, foundational rights/ethics, legal analysis, writing, realistic research, and basic fact patterns. Existing 85 authored lessons; full accuracy review underway. Graduation locked pending academic standards.
2. **1L**: contracts, torts, civil procedure, property, criminal law, constitutional law, legal research/writing. Build systematic element-by-element rules, issue spotting and evidence-supported reasoning.
3. **2L**: evidence, business associations, criminal procedure, family law, advanced constitutional/statutory analysis, and integrated writing/research.
4. **3L / Practical lawyering**: client counseling, investigation, negotiation, drafting, professional responsibility, simulated files and cross-subject case problems, plus Texas-specific doctrines.
5. **Bar Readiness**: mapped coverage of the current official NextGen blueprint plus Texas-specific graded component, structured remediation, timed multiple-choice and integrated formats, realistic writing and performance tasks, comprehensive independent simulated exams.

## Non-negotiable assessment safeguards
- Each assessment must link a blueprint objective and a reviewed source; record author, reviewer, review date, jurisdiction, and corrections.
- Prevent answer-position patterns, repeated questions disguised as new items, answer leakage and inflated progress based on clicks.
- Separate practice feedback from a **held-out** final assessment with unseen problems. Grade legal accuracy, application, counterarguments, sources, organization and task completion—not keywords or word count alone.
- Require multiple realistic full-length mock exams, timing controls, post-exam diagnostics and independently evaluated written/performance work. Compare score distributions to published passing expectations only when an evidence-based and defensible calibration exists. Never claim that a homemade percent score corresponds to an NCBE scaled score.
- Set a candidate-readiness decision only after repeated passing-standard performance under exam-like conditions, independently reviewed written work, and no critical subject gaps. Readiness is a prediction with uncertainty, never a guarantee.
- Maintain completion and student-progress state separate from any academic credential or graduation badge until all criteria are met.
- Update blueprint and Texas component regularly as NCBE and Texas publish changes.

## Eligibility disclaimer
Completing this app alone is not qualifying legal education and does not establish eligibility to register for any state bar or satisfy character-and-fitness, MPRE, or other jurisdiction-specific requirements. Academic exam readiness and legal eligibility are separate questions.
