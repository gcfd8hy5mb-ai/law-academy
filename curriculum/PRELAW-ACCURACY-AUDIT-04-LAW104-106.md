## LAW 105 — Everyday Legal Problems (4 lessons)
- law105-1 (quiz answer 1): Contract need not always be signed. **Improvement needed:** formation overview should also expressly mention legal capacity and lawful purpose, and recognized substitutes for consideration; verify applicable state statutes of frauds and exceptions. Source: Cornell LII https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/contract and https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/statute_of_frauds
- law105-2 (quiz answer 1): Duty is a negligence element, while premises duties and comparative responsibility vary by state. Retain.
- law105-3 (quiz answer 0): Presumption of innocence and proof beyond a reasonable doubt are correctly distinguished from accusation; retain.
- law105-4 (quiz answer 1): Property jurisdiction and governing state/local law must be identified. Retain.

## LAW 106 — Introduction to Legal Ethics (4 lessons)
- law106-1 (quiz answer 1): Lawyer diligence is a professional duty; ABA Model Rules are not automatically state-adopted. Retain. See ABA Rule 1.3.
- law106-2 (quiz answer 1): Confidentiality under Model Rule 1.6 is broader than evidentiary attorney-client privilege. Retain. Source: https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/
- law106-3 (quiz answer 1): Identify and evaluate conflicts, not all can be waived. Retain; local adopted rules control. See ABA Rule 1.7.
- law106-4 (quiz answer 1): Fabricated citations should be corrected, and candor to courts matters. Retain; Rule 3.3 and local procedural requirements may apply.
