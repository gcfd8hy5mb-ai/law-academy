# Level 1 Assessment Expansion — Batch 2

## Implemented
- Two additional original fact-pattern questions for each of six Pre-Law courses (12 total).
- Course practice exams include lesson checks plus two distinct case questions.
- Progress totals now cover every Pre-Law course instead of LAW 101 alone.
- Progress distinguishes lesson checks, course practice scores, cumulative practice exam, writing drafts, and unavailable evaluated assessments.
- Level 1 graduation remains unavailable pending defensible assessment of written legal reasoning.

## Quality limitations
These exercises are introductory, not comparable to a complete law-school final. Further work must add lengthy issue-spotting, primary legal opinions, source checking, rigorously reviewed writing rubrics, and substantive evaluation.

## Data preservation
Existing progress, exam, capstone and writing browser-storage keys remain unchanged. No user data reset was introduced.
