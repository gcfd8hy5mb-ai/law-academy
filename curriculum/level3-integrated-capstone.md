# Level 3 — Integrated Second-Year Law Capstone
Status: Authored assessment specification; **not graded, independently verified, or academically approved**.

## Case file: Asterion Medical Systems (fictional)
Asterion, a Delaware corporation, sells networked diagnostic devices across the United States. A company engineer reports that some devices inaccurately record patient readings. The CEO asks outside counsel to prepare a confidential report, but a public-relations team later circulates a summary containing disputed statements. The board authorizes a supplier acquisition involving a director's relative. A federal regulator orders an immediate software change without a published proposed rule. The company places a device on the market despite the controversy, is sued, and seeks an injunction to prevent a departing employee from disclosing a customer list. The employee is separately questioned by investigators after an arrest, and a prosecutor obtains messages from the employee's seized phone. An asset sale with installment payments creates a basis and tax-character dispute.

### Files provided to the student (fictional training record)
1. Board minutes recording the director's abstention but not the valuation discussion.
2. Device test log with incomplete chain of custody and an analyst using an undocumented statistical method.
3. Attorney interview notes and a nonlegal public-relations summary with alleged quotations from employees.
4. Agency bulletin, with no notice-and-comment record attached, mandating installation within 14 days.
5. Draft acquisition agreement, confidentiality terms, and supplier payment schedule.
6. Employee computer-access log, post-arrest interview note, and a phone-extraction timestamp before issuance of any warrant.
7. Preliminary revenue forecast, historical gross margin schedule, and cash-sale versus installment-sale term sheet.

## Required submissions
**Evidence (20 points).** Write an admissibility matrix for the test log, quoted employee statements, expert estimate, board minutes and phone extraction. Evaluate authentication, embedded hearsay, expert reliability, probative value and potential Rule 403 issues. Distinguish admissibility from evidentiary weight.

**Business Organizations (15 points).** Advise the board on duty of loyalty, care, conflict handling, corporate authority and shareholder remedies. Identify applicable incorporation law and material missing details.

**Administrative Law (15 points).** Analyze rule classification, statutory authority, possible notice-and-comment exceptions, standing, final agency action and forms of judicial review. Address post-Loper Bright statutory interpretation as appropriate.

**Professional Responsibility (10 points).** Identify the organization's client, interview warnings, confidential information, conflict risks, candor and permitted disclosures. Apply the jurisdiction's adopted professional rules.

**Federal Taxation (10 points).** Prepare a conditional amount-realized and adjusted-basis worksheet, assess installment-method eligibility and identify unverified year-specific assumptions. Do not invent the tax due.

**Remedies and Litigation (15 points).** Draft a narrowly tailored injunction and opposition. Evaluate likelihood of success, irreparable harm, balance of equities, public interest, evidence preservation, and quantifiable loss.

**Criminal Procedure (15 points).** Separate warrantless digital search, custodial interrogation, exclusion remedies and disclosure obligations. Identify attachment of counsel rights only if facts support it.

## Examination format
- 5,000–7,000 total written words plus charts/worksheets, divided among seven sections.
- Minimum 14 primary-law sources, including controlling authorities and current statutes/rules verified by actual text.
- Five fact changes requiring revised advice: conflicted director discloses fully; regulator claims concrete good cause; company withholds the PR summary; investigators obtain a phone warrant before examination; installment note is contingent.
- 20-minute simulated hearing: five-minute opening, ten-minute hostile questions, five-minute rebuttal.
- Required independent work: issue tree, detailed rule statements, factual applications, adverse cases, proposed disposition, source audit and revision log.

## Completion gate
A qualified reviewer must independently score each course-final written examination as well as this integrated capstone, examine primary-law accuracy and record revisions. The app's quiz completion or automatic keyword feedback cannot satisfy the completion gate. A reviewer should confirm the scenario is internally coherent and does not imply unsupported tax results, universally applicable ethics rules or automatic evidentiary outcomes.

The capstone is currently a written assignment specification, **not yet a fully interactive case-file simulation with live grading**. Neither this coursework nor self-study completion establishes accredited law-school credit, JD status, or bar exam eligibility.
