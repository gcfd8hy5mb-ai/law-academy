# Level 2 (1L) — completion and academic review gate

## Current authored inventory
Seven first-year subjects: Contracts, Torts, Civil Procedure, Criminal Law, Constitutional Law, Property, and Legal Research & Writing. As of this development batch: 121 authored lessons (previously 114), including one new subject-wide capstone per course, plus a seven-part integrated written examination. The opening doctrinal lesson of each course has been expanded with worked analysis, counterarguments and a written task.

## What the repository checks establish
The code validator and automated browser checks can confirm lesson presence, field structure, controls, runtime behavior and navigation. They **cannot** certify that the lessons have law-school-equivalent reading loads or that legal doctrine, authorities, marking and subject coverage have undergone independent expert scrutiny.

## Required before labeling Year 1 academically complete
- Map every lesson against a full 1L syllabus in its subject, with doctrinal subtopics and missing areas identified; include conflicts, agency, professional responsibility and evidence only where the intended year-one curriculum places them.
- Audit every judicial citation against the original opinion and check later history. Fix any inaccurate holding summaries or jurisdiction-dependent statements.
- Expand compressed one-paragraph lessons into sustained explanations, rule synthesis, competing doctrinal approaches, worked IRAC/CREAC and multiple application questions.
- Provide readings and cases with realistic estimated workload based on actual assigned material, not inferred hours from lesson count.
- Evaluate seven capstones and the integrated exam with robust model answers, clear scoring criteria and human review for substantive written work.
- Verify data persistence, navigation and accessibility on iPhone and desktop browsers.
- Seek an independent qualified legal educator to review the finished materials before describing the curriculum as equivalent to a first year of an accredited law school.

## Student completion rules
Answering a single three-option knowledge check marks that check only, not mastery of a subject. Written answers are stored as practice drafts unless explicitly graded by a qualified reviewer. A final-year completion designation should require documented evidence for each course, not merely passing its small self-check.

## Next editorial workflow
Prioritize lesson depth and case accuracy in groups of seven subject chapters. Track each chapter through source review, exercises, assessment and external review. Keep the existing 121 lessons intact while revising them. This document is a development gate, **not a declaration of academic completion**.
